Why the baseline model? (Cross-Entropy vs. Class-Weighted vs. Focal Loss)

OASIS is heavily imbalanced (test set: 76% Non Demented, 16% Very mild, 5.5% Mild, 1.8% Moderate; 13,237 slices from 53 held-out subjects, split by subject). We compared three losses on the same subject-level split. Per-class recall:

ModelNonVery mildMildModerateAccuracyMacro F1
Cross-Entropy (deployed)0.880.490.110.000.760.37
Class-Weighted CE0.850.430.090.000.730.34
Focal Loss (γ=2, α-weighted)0.960.070.030.010.750.26

Focal Loss reached 0.67 precision on Moderate, but only by predicting it very rarely (1% recall), and it pushed most Very mild cases into Non Demented. The baseline keeps the most balanced recall and the best macro F1, so it is the live model. Minority stages (Mild, Moderate) remain hard for every variant — treat those predictions with extra caution.
